THE POINT

Your ranking.
Not theirs.

AP Poll Sucks uses a balanced, four-part power model. Scores are standardized against the FBS field, then combined using the weights below. We compare the resulting order with ESPN’s Football Power Index (FPI) as an external benchmark; FPI does not feed back into our score.

Season-adjusted weights update automatically as more games are played.

ROSTER TALENT—ON-FIELD PERFORMANCE—STRENGTH OF SCHEDULE—RECENT FORM (LAST 4)—

Talent has more influence early in the season. Its weight gradually declines as on-field performance and schedule strength become more informative. Weights transition smoothly between season-week checkpoints. FPI is an external benchmark and does not feed back into our score. Missing optional data is treated neutrally rather than breaking the rankings update.
