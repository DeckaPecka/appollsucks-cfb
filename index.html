THE POINT

Your ranking.
Not theirs.

Each rating estimates how many points a team would score above or below an average FBS team on a neutral field. The difference between two teams’ ratings is the model’s projected neutral-field margin. For example, a +12 team would be projected about 7 points better than a +5 team on a neutral field.

The model fits results across the full network of FBS opponents, adjusting for opponent strength automatically rather than adding a separate schedule bonus. It removes home-field advantage from non-neutral games and caps extreme margins. Roster talent is only an early-season prior and fades as games accumulate. ESPN’s Football Power Index (FPI) is an external comparison only; it does not feed back into our rating.
